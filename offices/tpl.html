    Groups and other places to look

    Your chats

    Searched WhatsApp group chats for office, coworking, desk, Büro, Arbeitsplatz, sublet, office space and escritório. No Zurich office room on offer yet; the useful lead is the J floor ZH community.

    • J floor ZH (WhatsApp community): free founder base, no fees, no equity. Apply at thejfloor.com. Good place to ask if a member has a spare room.
    • YC Founders - Europe and YC Brazil (WhatsApp): ask whether a Zurich founder has a spare room or desks.
    • ETH groups: the ETH Student Project House gives ETH students free coworking (sph.ethz.ch).

    Other spaces (from web search, prices not confirmed on their sites)

    • Büro Züri by ZKB: free desk bookings at Bahnhofstrasse 9, plus a free startup programme at Innovation Park Dübendorf (buero-zueri.ch).
    • Impact Hub Zürich (Colab, Viadukt, Kraftwerk): Starter CHF 155 per person for 5 days a month, fixed desk CHF 595 per person (zurich.impacthub.ch).
    • Dynamo Raumbörse and Kulturbüro: cheap interim rooms, mostly for cultural projects.
