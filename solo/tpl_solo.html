        Tips

        • Student housing (WOKO, JUWO): ETH and UZH students can apply for rooms and studios from Zurich's student housing foundations. Not checked yet.
        • Cooperatives (Baugenossenschaften): like the Leimbach flat, cooperative flats are usually cheaper and the lease is open-ended; you become a member.
        • Flatfox Priority: some ads only let you write after 10–14 days unless you pay for Priority. Popular cheap flats are often gone by then.
        • Manegg: the S4 from Bahnhof Manegg reaches Zürich HB in a few minutes, so Leimbach and Wollishofen work for both.
