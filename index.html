      Market context

      • How commute times were measured: for every flat I asked the Swiss public-transport timetable (SBB data via transport.opendata.ch) for the fastest connection on a weekday morning around 08:00, door to door from the flat: to Zurich HB, to Home (Hottingen) and to St. Gallen station. The way back takes about the same. Times that could not be looked up show as "not looked up"; nothing on this page is estimated.
      • Swiss room counting: the .5 is the living area or a large hall. 1.5 rooms = one room plus kitchen, 2.5 rooms = living room + 1 bedroom + kitchen, 3.5 rooms = living room + 2 bedrooms.
      • For a couple, 2 to 2.5 rooms is the sweet spot: a separate bedroom costs only about CHF 200-250/month more than 1.5 rooms at the median, and the price per m2 drops from about CHF 51 to 38. 1.5-room flats are the most expensive per m2 and tight for two.
      • 3 to 3.5 rooms under CHF 2,500 exist but are rare near the centre; the real market median for 3.5 rooms is about CHF 3,000-3,400.
      • Official averages (Statistik Stadt Zurich, existing contracts, net rent): 2 rooms about CHF 1,415 (Kreis 12) to 2,194 (Kreis 1); 3 rooms about CHF 1,576 (Kreis 12) to 2,855 (Kreis 1). Newly advertised flats run higher: about CHF 2,700-2,850 for 2.5 rooms on average, which is why cheap ads get dozens of applications.
      • St. Gallen vs Zurich: asking rents are about CHF 20-23 per m2 per month in St. Gallen vs CHF 35-40 in Zurich, so roughly 50-70% more. A 70 m2 flat at about CHF 1,600 in St. Gallen costs about CHF 2,600-2,800 in Zurich.
      • Central costs less extra than you'd think: within this budget, flats under 2 km from HB cost only about CHF 150-250/month more at the median, but there are far fewer of them. Allowing 2-5 km (Oerlikon, Unterstrass 8057, Wiedikon, Wipkingen, Altstetten, all with S-Bahn or tram to HB in under 10 minutes) roughly triples the choice.
      • Lease length: this report keeps only open-ended leases (unbefristet) and fixed-term leases that run past 31 Dec 2027. 134 short sublets / temporary leases and 109 ads with an unclear end date or a move-in after April 2027 were removed. Most Zurich flats around CHF 1,500 near the centre turned out to be short sublets.

      How to apply, step by step

      1. Build the dossier now, before any viewing (one PDF per person, or one joint PDF): short cover letter (template below), ID or residence permit, last 3 payslips or work contracts, a Betreibungsauszug (debt-record extract, under 3 months old), proof of liability insurance (Privathaftpflicht), and a reference from the current St. Gallen landlord. Rule of thumb: rent should be at most 1/3 of joint gross income, so CHF 2,000 rent needs about CHF 6,000/month gross together.
      2. Order the Betreibungsauszug online from the debt-collection office of their current address (St. Gallen), about CHF 17, arrives in a few days: EasyGov.swiss (search Betreibungsauszug) or via the Comparis e-Betreibungsauszug service.
      3. Set alerts on every portal so they see new ads within minutes: Flatfox, Homegate, ImmoScout24, Newhome, UrbanHome, Comparis (Suchabo button on the search above). Also Facebook groups like Wohnungen Zurich for Nachmieter hand-overs.
      4. Apply the same day. On Flatfox, tap Apply on the ad (the Apply buttons in this report go straight there), fill the online form and attach the dossier; many landlords only invite the first applicants to a viewing. On Homegate / ImmoScout24 use the Contact or Apply button, or call the number in the ad.
      5. At the viewing: bring printed dossiers, ask about Nebenkosten, the move-in date and how many applicants there are, and send the application form the same evening. Visit street-facing flats (Langstrasse, Rosengartenstrasse, Hardbruecke) at night to check noise.
      6. Cooperatives (Genossenschaften), often 20-40% below market: most waiting lists are closed, so watch their vacancy pages and apply immediately: ABZ, ASIG, BEP, Kalkbreite, Kraftwerk1, Mehr als Wohnen, Stiftung PWG, the regional association wbg Zurich (list of all cooperatives) and city-owned flats on stadt-zuerich.ch (search Liegenschaften Wohnungen). Expect share capital (Anteilscheine, often CHF 1,000-10,000, refunded when you leave) and occupancy rules.
      7. Money to have ready: deposit up to 3 months' net rent (about CHF 4,500-6,000) in a blocked rent-deposit account at a bank, or a deposit insurance instead: SwissCaution, FirstCaution (about 5% of the deposit per year). Plus first month's rent, moving van, household insurance about CHF 150-300/year and the Serafe fee CHF 335/year per household.
      8. Leaving St. Gallen: standard notice is 3 months to an official moving date in their contract. Proposing a suitable replacement tenant (Nachmieter) lets them leave early.
      9. After moving: register at the Kreisbuero within 14 days (stadt-zuerich.ch, search Zuzug). A ZVV city pass (zone 110) is about CHF 85/month each: zvv.ch. Tenant rights and rent-check help: Mieterverband.
      10. Avoid scams: never pay a deposit or fee before a viewing and a signed contract; be wary of very cheap central flats where the owner is abroad.

      Groups and channels to join

      Places where people post flats, Nachmieter hand-overs and sublets. Group names come from web searches and could not all be opened, so search the name if a link does not land on the group. In every channel, skip ads that say befristet, Untermiete or Zwischenmiete unless they run past 2027, and never pay before a viewing.

      Facebook groups (biggest source of Nachmieter hand-overs)

      • Wohnung Zürich / Wohnungen Zürich: several large groups with this name
      • Nachmieter Zürich / Nachmietergesuche Zürich: tenants leaving and looking for someone to take over their lease (often the cheapest long-term flats)
      • Zurich Housing, Rooms, Apartments, Sublets
      • Flats in Zurich / Zurich Apartments
      • Expats in Zurich: general, has frequent housing posts

      Reddit

      • r/zurich: search "apartment" or "Nachmieter"; occasional hand-over posts and lots of search tips
      • r/askswitzerland and r/ethz: housing questions and offers

      Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord

      • Mein Zürich Heim (Telegram): bot that posted new cooperative flats; its latest posts are from 2023, so it looks inactive
      • WhatsApp and Discord: no public Zurich housing group could be found. These are invite-only, usually run by student associations (VSETH at ETH, VSUZH at UZH), churches and expat communities. Asking in those circles is the way in.

      Newsletters and alerts (check daily)

      • GenossenSchaffen: emails every new cooperative flat from 100+ cooperatives, CHF 5/month
      • immomailing: daily 17:30 newsletter of Zurich flats and Nachmieter offers, CHF 49 once
      • Ron Orp: free daily Zurich newsletter with a housing market
      • Search alerts on Flatfox, Homegate, ImmoScout24, UrbanHome and Comparis (Suchabo): turn on push notifications and apply within hours
