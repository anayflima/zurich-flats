All three close together

Groups where a flat for two, a cheap flat for one (max CHF ) and an office for two are all within min walking of each other. Built from the three pages: Flats for two · Flats for one · Office rooms. Updated .

Each place passes its own page's rules: flat for two = 2+ rooms, up to CHF 2,500, long lease, not flagged as suspicious; flat for one = up to CHF 900; office = a private room just for the two of you, whole cost clear and up to CHF 500. "Almost" groups miss one office rule: desks in a shared office (up to CHF 500 for both) or a private room up to CHF 800. Walking times are real OpenStreetMap foot routes between the listed locations; transit times come from the timetable lookups on each page. Some listings only give a street or area, so their pin is approximate (marked ≈).
